What you get

Three ways to score

Your watch, your phone, or a cheap Bluetooth remote in your pocket. Whichever is easiest without breaking play.

Four sports, real rules

Padel, tennis, badminton and pickleball — each scored the way its own rulebook says, not a generic counter.

Straight into Apple Health

Every match is saved as a proper workout with heart rate, calories and duration.

Let people watch

Mirror the score to a friend's phone beside you, or send a link so anyone can follow from home.

Know your game

History, per-player win rates and long-term trends — plus a recap after every match.

Speaks your language

English, Suomi, Español, Deutsch and עברית — in the app and in the spoken score.

Private by design

No accounts. No analytics. No tracking. Your matches and Health data stay on your devices, and back up only to your own iCloud.
